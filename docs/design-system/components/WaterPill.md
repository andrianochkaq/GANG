# WaterPill

The water shortcut in the header (36 px white pill, drop icon, «1,2 / 2 л» in `water-ink`); one tap adds 250 ml. Shown only when water balance is on and «Показувати в шапці» is checked.

- Desktop: 44 px with the action in the label: «1,2 / 2 л · +250 мл».
- `aria-label`: «Вода: 1,2 з 2 літрів. Додати 250 мл». After a tap, the number updates and a toast offers «Скасувати».
- The full water page uses the fixed water colours (`water-bg`, `water-fill`, `water-text`) — water stays blue in every theme.
- Avatars in the same row: 36 px circle with initials (2 letters, 13 / 600); 44 px in member lists; 52 px on `soft` in the profile header.
